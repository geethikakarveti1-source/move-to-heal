Educational Resources & Guides

Learn how to perform your assigned exercises safely to optimize your recovery and avoid injuries.

Squat

Bodyweight Squat

Target: Lower Body (Quadriceps, Gluteals, Knee Stability)

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes pointing slightly outward.
  • Lower your hips back and down as if sitting in an imaginary chair.
  • Keep your knees aligned with your toes; do not let them collapse inward (valgus).
  • Lower until your thighs are parallel to the ground (knee angle around 90-100°).
  • Push through your heels to return to the starting position.
Critical Guardrails: Do not arch your spine, and ensure knees do not bow inward.
Bicep Curl

Bicep Curl

Target: Upper Body (Elbow Flexion, Bicep Brachii)

  • Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing forward.
  • Keep your elbows close to your torso at all times.
  • Curl the weights upward while contracting your biceps.
  • Continue raising until biceps are fully contracted (elbow angle near 30-40°).
  • Slowly lower the weights back to the starting position (full extension, 170-180°).
Critical Guardrails: Do not swing your body or rely on momentum. Keep upper arms stationary.
Shoulder Press

Shoulder Press

Target: Deltoids, Rotator Cuff, Upper Elbow Extension

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding weights at shoulder level.
  • Engage your core and keep your chest upright.
  • Press the weights straight up above your head until arms are fully extended.
  • Lower the weights back down slowly, keeping elbows in front of body.
Critical Guardrails: Do not arch your lower back. Control the descent weight.
Lunge

Forward Lunge

Target: Lower Body (Quadriceps, Gluteals, Hip Flexors, Alignment)

  • Step forward with one leg, keeping your torso vertical.
  • Lower your body until your rear knee is close to the floor.
  • Ensure your front knee does not pass past your toes.
  • Keep your hips level and stable throughout the motion.
  • Push back up with your front leg to return to standing.
Critical Guardrails: Maintain core stability. Avoid torso leaning too far forward.
Lateral Raise

Lateral Shoulder Raise

Target: Deltoids, Rotator Cuff Abduction Strength

  • Stand tall with dumbbells at your sides, core engaged.
  • Raise your arms out to the sides with a slight elbow bend.
  • Stop once your arms are parallel to the floor (shoulder level, 90°).
  • Slowly lower the weights back to the starting position.
Critical Guardrails: Do not raise arms above shoulder line. Control the speed.
Wall Sit

Wall Sit

Target: Quadriceps, Glutes, Knee Stability, Endurance

  • Stand with your back against a wall.
  • Slide down until knees reach approximately 90°.
  • Keep knees aligned with toes.
  • Maintain a neutral spine.
  • Hold for 20–60 seconds.
Critical Guardrails: Do not let knees collapse inward. Keep lower back against the wall. Stop if knee pain occurs.
